Privacy Policy

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! — Scam Checker

Last updated: 3 October 2026

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! is designed to help users identify warning signs in suspicious messages, links, screenshots and QR codes before taking action. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the service.

Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, you may submit text messages, website links, screenshots, QR codes, images, or additional context for analysis. You should avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

How information is used

Information you submit is processed only as necessary to provide scam-risk analysis, identify warning signs, and return relevant safety guidance.

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! does not intentionally store the messages, call descriptions, links, screenshots or QR contents you submit on its servers after processing. Information is held in memory only as needed to perform the check, subject to the third-party processing described below.

Third-party processing

Submitted content may be securely processed using OpenAI services to generate scam-risk analysis. Website addresses, including web addresses decoded from QR codes, may also be sent to Google Web Risk to identify known potentially harmful URLs. These third-party services operate under their own data practices and retention policies.

Screenshots and images

Uploaded screenshots and images are processed for analysis. STOP! CHECK! WAIT! does not intentionally maintain a server-side history of uploaded image files.

Check history

The service may keep a limited history of recent checks locally on your device or browser for your convenience. This local history is not an account-based cloud history and can be removed by clearing the relevant app or browser data.

Accounts

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! currently does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its scam-checking features.

Advertising

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! currently does not display third-party advertising within the scam checker.

Data security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information during processing. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

STOP! CHECK! WAIT! is not specifically designed for or directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service develops or its features and data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on the STOP! CHECK! WAIT! Google Play listing.
